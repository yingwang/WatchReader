Press and hold a word to highlight it in one of four colours, add a note, look it up, translate, copy or share it; each book keeps a list of highlights and notes to jump back to or export. The phone now reads books aloud, with speed, a sleep timer, voices named female and male with a preview, and a bar on every screen to pause or return to the book. Auto page turn has its own panel: choose slow to fast, tap Start, and a small sign shows it is on.
