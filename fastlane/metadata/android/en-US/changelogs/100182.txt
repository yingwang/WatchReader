Reading aloud now recognises 26 languages: Czech, Turkish, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Ukrainian join them, and Czech is no longer taken for Polish. A voice the watch is missing is downloaded as soon as a book arrives that needs it; if it cannot be fetched, reading pauses and says why. A language the watch has no voice for is skipped with a notice instead of being read in the wrong voice, and Settings shows which voices are installed, downloaded on first use, or unavailable.
