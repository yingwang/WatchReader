Automatic page turning. Long-press while reading to open the panel and switch on Auto; pages then turn by themselves, eight seconds each until you set the pace in Settings, anywhere from one second to thirty-one. It waits whenever you leave the page, so a book left open with the screen off stays where you left it and your place on the phone is not moved. Read aloud switches it off, since the voice turns pages on its own. The crown turns pages whether or not Auto is running.
