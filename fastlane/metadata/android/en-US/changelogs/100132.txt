The chapters shown on the watch now come from the phone, which reads them out of the book itself. The watch used to work them out from the text, and a book that prints its own contents defeated that: the printed list became the chapters, so every one led back to the first page. Reading the contents on the phone is fixed as well, for contents pages, for books kept as a single file, and for blank entries. A book sent to the watch before this needs sending again.
