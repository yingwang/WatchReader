A new layout: Library, Discover and Stats sit in a bar at the bottom. In your library you can search by title, show only the books you are reading, have finished or have on your watch, sort them, and switch between covers and a list. Add several books at once, then start reading or send them all to your watch. A book sent while the watch is away waits and goes over once it is back. Bookmarks, kept in step with the watch, and search inside a book. Stats show your days in a row.
