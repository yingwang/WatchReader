WatchReader turns your Wear OS watch into a book reader.

How it works
Add a book on the phone: tap the plus button in the library to add a file or a link, several at once if you like, or share a book to WatchReader from another app. Tap Discover at the bottom of the phone screen for free classics from Project Gutenberg.

Send it to the watch: open the book on the phone and tap the send button at the top, or the three dots on the cover and Send to watch. The watch then has its own copy.

Open it on the watch: tap the book in the watch library (next time, opening the app on the watch goes straight back into it). Turn pages with a tap (right half forward, left half back) or the crown. Press and hold the page for the reading controls. Tap the triangle to listen (a double tap on the page pauses, the square stops). Tap Contents in the controls to jump to a chapter. To delete a book, press and hold it in the library.

Features
• Read on your watch, turning pages with the crown or a tap
• Read aloud on the watch or the phone, in the book's own language, carrying on with the screen off, with a sleep timer
• Auto page turn on the watch and the phone, from slow to fast
• Bookmarks on the watch and the phone, kept in step
• Add books from a file, a link, or by sharing, in txt, epub and fb2
• Free classics from Project Gutenberg
• Read on your phone as well, with your place kept in step
• Highlight and add notes on your phone, look up or translate a word, and export your notes
• Your choice of font, text size, line spacing and margins on the phone
• Search your library, and the text of a book, on your phone
• See how long you have read on your phone
• No account and no ads

Requires a phone running the companion app and a watch on Wear OS 3 or later.
